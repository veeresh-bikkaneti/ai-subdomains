# Code of Conduct

This is a small volunteer project. We want it to stay friendly and low-drama.

## Our standard

* Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers making their first PR.
* Assume good faith, give clear and kind feedback, and take feedback gracefully.
* Keep discussion on topic: names, records, rules and the code.
* Respect maintainers' time. Reviews happen when volunteers are free; don't ping, nag or open duplicate PRs to speed things up.

## Not acceptable

* Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, or sexualised language.
* Doxxing or sharing others' private information.
* Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or bad-faith abuse reports.
* Trying to exploit the pipeline, hide behaviour in obfuscated code, or evade checks and limits.
* Using the service in ways forbidden by the [Terms](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close PRs and issues, ban people from the repository, and revoke subdomains. Report a problem privately to `security@is-ai.si` or via GitHub's "report content" feature; reports about maintainers can go to any other maintainer. We will keep reporters' identities confidential where we can.

This Code of Conduct applies in this repository and when representing it in public.
